About Security Deposits

Your security deposit is held to cover:

  • Unpaid rent at the end of tenancy
  • Damage to the property beyond normal wear and tear
  • Cleaning costs if room is left in poor condition
  • Unpaid utility bills
  • Other breaches of tenancy agreement
Keep your room in good condition and pay all bills on time to ensure full deposit refund!
